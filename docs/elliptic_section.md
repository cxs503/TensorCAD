## 1. Closed form

For a prism of elliptic cross-section (semi-axes `a` transverse, `b` vertical) floating at
half immersion, the immersed section at every heel is an exact half-ellipse, so

    KN(phi) = b sin(phi) + (4/(3 pi)) (a^2 - b^2) sin(phi) cos(phi) / sqrt(a^2 sin^2 + b^2 cos^2)

    GZ(phi) = KN(phi) - KG sin(phi)

with `KG = 0.4 m` from the keel and `L = 4.0 m`.
