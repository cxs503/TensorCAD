# Mesh topology and face quality: checked against trimesh and closed forms

> Generated by `tools/mesh_quality_topology_xval.py` from

> number below is injected. Figure: `artifacts/mesh_quality_topology/plot.png`.

## 1. What was missing

`tensorcad.meshquality` measured cells. It did not answer the questions that
actually reject a mesh: is the surface closed, is every edge used by exactly two
faces *in opposite directions*, are the faces wound outward, is a vertex pinched,
is a face duplicated, and how far are the internal faces from orthogonal? The
repository answered parts of this in `tensorcad.mesh.py` (watertight, edge-manifold)
and `tensorcad.validation` (a sliver share), with no shared vocabulary, no
vertex-manifold or duplicate-face check, no Euler/genus, and nothing gating it. The
equiangle skewness that commercial solvers gate on was absent everywhere.

`meshquality.topology` adds exactly those: `surface_topology()` (closure,
edge/vertex manifoldness, duplicate faces, winding, outward orientation, Euler
characteristic, shells, genus, signed volume) and `non_orthogonality()` (the
OpenFOAM internal-face quantity for `hex`/`tet` meshes), plus the `skewness` metric
in all four element kinds and `--topology` / `--max-non-orth` / `--max-genus` on
the CLI, which exit 1 on a failed gate.

## 5. Skewness

Over the 1998 triangles of the default hull the library and the
independent route differ by at most 1.332e-15 (mean
2.935e-16). Hull skewness: min 0.0659766, mean
0.677703, max 0.881123 (0 = ideal,
1 = degenerate).

Documented blind spot, also asserted in the tests: a box stretched 4:1 in x scores
skewness 0 and scaled Jacobian 1
— both shape-only family metrics are blind to anisotropy — while
`min_sicn` 0.419974 and `edge_ratio` 4
see it. Skewness is therefore reported *next to* them, never instead of them.

## 6. Points to watch

* Non-orthogonality uses node centroids as cell centres. That is exact for the
  simplices and for affine hexahedra (hence the exact closed-form agreement) and is
  the documented approximation for warped cells; OpenFOAM integrates the true
  polyhedral centroid, so a strongly warped cell can differ in the second decimal.
* Faces shared by three or more cells raise `ValueError` instead of being averaged:
  a non-conforming mesh has no unique centre-to-centre line, and silently averaging
  would hide the defect the metric exists to find. Duplicate cells (coincident
  centres) are counted as `degenerate_faces` and fail the gate.
* `trimesh`'s `volume` is signed too, so it validates the orientation verdict and not
  just closure; but its `euler_number` on *open* meshes counts boundary edges the
  same way this module does only by coincidence of definition — the agreement
  observed here (`open_box`, `non_manifold_fin`) is evidence, not a guarantee.
* `gmsh` and `meshmagick` remain unavailable on this loongarch64 host, so the
  Gmsh/minSICN comparison is still outstanding; the topology comparison uses trimesh,
  which *is* importable here.

## 7. Verdict

* 9 surfaces, 2595 faces: the library agrees
  with trimesh on every boolean, Euler number, shell count and signed volume
  (worst |dV| 6.217e-15 m^3).
* Internal-face non-orthogonality reproduces the closed form to
  2.132e-14 deg over
  14 configurations.
* Triangle skewness reproduces its independent route to
  1.332e-15.
* Key quantities vs their benchmarks are all far below the 3 % criterion (they are
  round-off level, < 1e-12 relative), so the <3 % question is answered yes with a
  large margin; the outstanding item is the Gmsh `minSICN`/`gamma` comparison, which
  cannot run on this host and is therefore recorded as not run, not as passed.
* The gate is usable in CI: `--topology --max-genus 0 --max-non-orth 70` exits 1 on
  an open, inward-wound, non-manifold or badly skewed mesh (see
  `tests/test_meshquality_topology.py`).
