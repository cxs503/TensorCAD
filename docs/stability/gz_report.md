## Scope

* No free-surface correction, no damage stability, no statutory criterion (`IS Code 2008`, weather criterion) and no downflooding opening: the arm is a solid-body, single-compartment quantity.
* The body handed to the solver must be the *whole* closed body including freeboard. A wetted-surface mesh closed by a cap at the design waterline has zero freeboard, and heeling it drowns the deck: the arm is then negative and that is a statement about the body, not about the ship.
* Trim is not solved: the hull is held at even keel, so the arm is transverse only and `x_G` is not used.
