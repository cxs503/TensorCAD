## The shipped hull family has no large-angle curve

`generate_hull` caps the surface at the design waterline, so the displacement is the whole body volume and no heeled waterline can hold it. The module raises:

```
the requested displacement 7.446554097149238 m^3 is the whole volume of the body (7.446554097149227 m^3), so the body has no freeboard: a closed surface capped at its waterline cannot heel at constant displacement (any heel would have to hold the entire body below the surface, and the arm is then identically zero). Generate a deeper body and float it at a lower waterline_z, e.g. HullParameters(draft=2 * t) with waterline_z=-t
```
