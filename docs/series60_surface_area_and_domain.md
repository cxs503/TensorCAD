## 1. Case

* `L = 10.0 m`, `B = 1.5 m`, `T = 0.6 m`, water 1025.0 kg/m^3.
* Five published Todd parents, Cb = 0.60, 0.65, 0.70, 0.75, 0.80.
* Bars: wetted area and volume both 2 % against their own reference, the same bar
  the family already carries for volume.

## 2. Two debts and what was done about them

**2.1 The advertised Cb window was not reachable.** `Series60Parameters` and its
validator both said `Cb` in `[0.55, 0.85]`, but the published ladder ends at
`Cb = 0.80` with `Cp = 0.805`, and `Cm = Cb/Cp` needs `Cp > Cb`. Every request in
`(0.805, 0.85]` therefore died *inside* the interpolation with
`Cp (0.805000) must exceed Cb (0.850000)` -- a message that names neither the
parameter nor its range. The window is now the ladder itself
(`CB_MIN = 0.55`, `CB_MAX = 0.8`, derived from `_PARENT_FORMS`), and the
refusal names the ladder. The new test sweeps the whole window, boundary included,
because that is what an advertised range means.

**2.2 The wetted surface had no reference at all.** `wetted_surface_area` was the
one quantity in `NO_CLOSED_FORM` -- and it is the quantity resistance consumes.
`series60_surface_area_reference` now integrates the parametric metric two
independent ways: 2-D Gauss-Legendre on the analytic Jacobian (keel
singularity removed by the `zeta = u**k` map, `k >= max m(xi)`), and parametric
quads on an 80/160/320 ladder, Richardson-extrapolated. The mesh is compared
against that, not against itself.

integrals come in short, and both close at roughly `1/nz` -- the keel end of
`zeta**(1/m)` has unbounded slope, which is first order in the level spacing.

## 5. Notes and limits

* The reference is *not* external. It removes the tautology of measuring the mesh
  against itself; it does not anchor the family's shape to any published offset
  table, which remains WITHHELD (no network in this sandbox, no in-repo table).
* No bulb term enters the reference: a bulb is a separate closed solid, and its
  own wetted area is already reported by `series60_bulb` (quadrature with its own
  error estimate). The comparison here is the hull plus, when asked for, the
  immersed transom face.
* `CB_MAX` is the fullest *published* parent. Extrapolating the ladder past
  Cb = 0.80 would need a Cp the literature does not tabulate, so the window stops
  there instead of inventing one.

## 6. Error verdict

| quantity | reference | worst error at CLI grid | bar | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| wetted surface area | independent 2-D quadrature, two paths | 0.706 % | 2 % | PASS |
| displacement volume | closed form of the resolved law | 1.222 % | 2 % | PASS |

Both are **below 3 %** (0.706 % and 1.222 %),
and both are below the tighter 2 % bar the family declares. The error is
discretisation only: it is signed, it shrinks with the grid, and the reference it
is measured against opens no external door, which §5 states plainly.
